# T-205 · Rule review — the expert agrees with what we learned
**Lane C · owner: Olha · depends on: T-110, T-204**

Collect every rule learned during sessions (the knowledge graph facts from T-110, plus the steps and
guardrails confirmed in each Work Map) and show them to the expert (the instructor) as one list to
agree with. Each rule can be agreed, corrected or rejected.

Show each rule with where it came from: the verbatim quote, the session and the date. A rule the
expert has not agreed to is not yet company knowledge.

Open questions:

- Does the tutor (T-302) only teach agreed rules, the way it only loads a confirmed Work Map (T-204)?
- When two sessions disagree, show both side by side and let the expert pick one.
- Review after each debrief, or as a separate screen the expert opens when they want?

**Acceptance:** after two sessions, the expert sees one list with every rule learned in both, agrees
with some and corrects one, and the correction shows up in the knowledge graph.

# T-204 · Teach-back and confirmation
**Lane C · depends on: T-203**

The debrief ends by explaining the whole process back in the agent's own words, in **under a minute**,
and the expert confirms or corrects it. This is the moment that proves understanding, so it has to be a
synthesis — steps, judgment calls and guardrails in the order they matter — not a replay of the event
log.

Corrections must land as edits to the Work Map, not as extra transcript: the expert says "no, the second
approval is only over ten thousand", and the guardrail's statement changes. Show the diff on screen as it
happens.

On confirmation, stamp `confirmed_at` and freeze the map. Only a confirmed map can be loaded by the tutor
(T-300) — an unconfirmed map teaching a new hire is how wrong knowledge propagates.

**Acceptance:** the teach-back runs under 60s, the expert corrects at least one detail, that correction is
visible as a changed field in the Work Map, and the tutor refuses to load the map until it is confirmed.

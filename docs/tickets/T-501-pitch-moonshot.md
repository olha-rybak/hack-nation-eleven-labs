# T-501 · Pitch + moonshot slide
**Lane C · owner: Olha · depends on: T-500**

The brief asks the pitch to end on one moonshot slide: what you would build next and **how today's MVP
gets you there**. The weak-submission column names "a demo that stops at the demo", so the path matters
more than the ambition.

Our line, given the architecture: today one expert, one task, one new hire, and a Work Map that is already
a machine-readable object with provenance on every claim. The step from there to **a living company
memory** is quantitative, not architectural — many Work Maps, a diff when the work changes, and the
apprentice asks only about what is new. The always-on apprentice is the same capture loop without a
scheduled session.

Also prepare the agent-ready guardrails angle (T-602): the same `GuardrailCheck` objects that stop a new
hire are what let an agent take routine steps safely while people keep the judgment calls.

**Acceptance:** a deck that runs in the allotted time, ending on one slide with the moonshot and the three
concrete steps from the MVP to it.

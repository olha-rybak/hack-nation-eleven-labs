# T-303 · The unseen case + progress report
**Lane C · depends on: T-302, T-301**

The Teach module's `Required` is explicit: a judge playing a new hire processes a case **the expert never
showed**. Build it and prove the generalization.

The unseen case (INV-4474, held back in T-100): a €9,400 equipment invoice from a *new* supplier, which
is both over the capex line and an unknown vendor. The expert never processed this exact combination, so
the tutor has to compose two guardrails rather than match one — and a correct intervention here is the
difference between a lookup table and an apprentice.

The progress report at the end: steps completed correctly, guardrails hit and how they were resolved,
what to practice next. Keep it to one screen and make it specific — "you coded capex without an asset
number" beats a score.

**Acceptance:** a teammate who has not seen the Work Map plays the new hire, reaches for 4711 on
INV-4474, and the tutor stops them before Save with the expert's reasoning. The report names the
guardrail they missed.

# Screen: Return case (return-case.png)

Case RMA-1043 opened. The title shows the return number, its status badge and below it the item and
price. Left column, top to bottom: **Order** (Order no., Item, SKU, Price paid, Delivered, Item
opened, Packaging), **Request** (Reason and the customer's message in a box), **Customer** (name,
email, City, Customer since, and two counters: Orders (12 months) and Returns (12 months)). Right
column: **Decision** (Resolution dropdown, Refund amount (EUR), Restocking fee checkbox, Note, and
the buttons Save, Escalate to supervisor, Complete) and **History**. Refund amount and Restocking
fee are greyed out until Refund or Goodwill refund is chosen; Complete is disabled until a
resolution is chosen.

# Returns policy (written)

From the customer-service handbook, section "Returns", last revised 2024. This is everything that
is written down.

1. Customers can return any item within **30 days of delivery**. The delivery date is the date on
   the carrier's proof of delivery.
2. Items that are returned opened and are not defective are subject to a **restocking fee of 15 %**
   of the price paid.
3. **Refunds above 200 EUR** require supervisor approval. Use *Escalate to supervisor* with the
   reason "Refund over 200 EUR".
4. Defective items within the return window are always taken back free of charge.
5. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of completion.
